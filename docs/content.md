## How an appointment works
1. **Call or send a request.** Tell us what needs signing, how many people are signing and where you'd like to meet.
2. **Have documents and ID ready.** Leave the documents unsigned. Every signer needs a current government-issued photo ID.
3. **Meet, verify and sign.** We check each signer's ID, witness the signatures and complete the notarization on the spot.
